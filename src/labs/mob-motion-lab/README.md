# Mob Motion Lab

Open `lab.html?lab=mob-motion-lab` to compare three simulated animation clips on
approved generated enemy art:

- **Movement:** bob, squash/stretch, and directional lean.
- **Attack:** anticipation, forward lunge, and recovery.
- **Hit reaction:** recoil, shake, compression, fade, and white flash.

Choose any mobile enemy variant from the generated sprite manifest. Motion speed,
intensity, preview scale, and deterministic scrub time are adjustable with lil-gui.
The preview uses manifest hold and center-of-gravity anchors when available.
