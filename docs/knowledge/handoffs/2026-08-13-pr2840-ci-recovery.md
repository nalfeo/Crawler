## Retrospective

### Lessons Learned

The live mob-footprint E2E had two overlapping contracts: individual authored-height bands allowed ±20% render motion, but the aggregate ratio used a fixed threshold that was tighter than those bands when the samples landed at opposite ends.

### Mistakes Made

I initially treated the E2E failure as a pure provider-PR blocker, but the failing job was an unrelated live visual flake in the full CI surface; the early signal was that the local single-test run passed while the CI ratio only slightly exceeded a hard-coded threshold.

### Opportunities for Future Improvement

Consider replacing duplicated numeric authored heights in this E2E with values read from `entity-sprite-mappings.json`, matching the unit guard and reducing drift when mob sizing data changes.
